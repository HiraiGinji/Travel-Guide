Matsumoto Castle

Known as the "Crow Castle" for its black exterior, Matsumoto Castle is one of Japan's most beautiful and historically significant castles.

Explore Matsumoto Castle

One of Japan’s most iconic structures, Matsumoto Castle dates back to the late 16th century. Its imposing black walls stand out against the stunning backdrop of the Japanese Alps. The castle's main keep, or "tenshu," is designated as a National Treasure.

  • One of Japan’s oldest surviving castles
  • Offers breathtaking views of the Japanese Alps
  • Famous for its "crow-like" black exterior

Best Time to Visit

The best time to visit is during spring when the castle is surrounded by cherry blossoms, or in winter when the snow-capped peaks create a stunning contrast with the black castle.
