Shinjuku Gyoen

A peaceful green oasis in Tokyo, offering traditional gardens and seasonal beauty year-round.

Discover Shinjuku Gyoen

Located in the heart of Tokyo, Shinjuku Gyoen combines three distinct styles—Japanese traditional, English landscape, and French formal. It’s one of the city’s most serene and picturesque parks, perfect for escaping the hustle of urban life.

  • Home to over 1,000 cherry trees
  • Features a greenhouse with tropical and subtropical plants
  • Ideal for picnics, strolls, and seasonal photography

Best Time to Visit

Spring is the most popular season with its breathtaking cherry blossoms, but autumn offers stunning fall foliage as well. The garden remains beautiful and accessible year-round.
