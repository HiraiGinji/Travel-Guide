Tokyo Imperial Palace

The iconic residence of Japan’s Imperial Family, blending tradition and beauty in the heart of Tokyo.

Explore Tokyo Imperial Palace

The Tokyo Imperial Palace, located in the heart of Japan’s capital, serves as the official residence of the Emperor of Japan. It is a historical landmark, surrounded by beautiful gardens and fortifications. The palace complex includes the East Gardens, an exquisite spot to enjoy the serenity and beauty of nature within the city.

  • A symbol of Japan's royal heritage
  • Open to the public with guided tours available
  • Spectacular gardens and serene surroundings

Best Time to Visit

The Tokyo Imperial Palace is open to visitors year-round, but spring and autumn offer the most pleasant weather and the chance to experience the stunning seasonal changes in the gardens. The cherry blossoms in spring are particularly famous and attract many visitors.
