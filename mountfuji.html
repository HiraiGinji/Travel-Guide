Mount Fuji

An iconic peak of Japan, revered for its majestic beauty and cultural significance.

Explore Mount Fuji

Mount Fuji, Japan’s highest peak at 3,776 meters, is not only a stunning natural wonder but also a cultural icon deeply embedded in Japanese art, history, and spirituality. Whether you're hiking to the summit or admiring it from afar, Mount Fuji offers a unique experience across all seasons.

  • UNESCO World Heritage Site
  • Famous for hiking trails and sunrise views
  • Easily accessible from Tokyo via train or bus

Best Time to Visit

The official climbing season is from early July to early September when the weather is mild and mountain huts are open. However, for photographers and sightseers, autumn and winter provide the clearest views.
