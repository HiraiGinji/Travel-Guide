Plan Your Perfect Trip to Japan

Step-by-step guide and insider travel tips to explore Japan like never before.

Trip Planning Made Easy

Step 1: Documents Ready
  • Valid Passport
  • Visa (if needed)
  • Flight Tickets
  • Travel Insurance
Step 2: Book Essentials
  • Accommodation in Tokyo/Kyoto/Osaka
  • JR Rail Pass
  • Day tours and theme park tickets
Step 3: Packing Up
  • Comfortable shoes
  • Weather-appropriate clothes
  • Power bank & adapters
  • Cash in Yen
Step 4: Final Touches
  • Install navigation apps
  • Set emergency contacts
  • Download offline maps
  • Print bookings

Top Travel Tips in Japan

  • Always bow when greeting.
  • Remove shoes before entering homes or temples.
  • Speak softly in public transport.
Respect Local Customs

In Japan, respecting local traditions is essential for a positive experience.

Polite Behavior

Always be polite and aware of your surroundings, especially in public places.

  • Use an IC Card (Suica/Pasmo) for trains and buses.
  • Trains are punctual — be on time!
  • Google Maps is your best friend in Japan.
Punctual Trains

Japan's trains run on time, so make sure to arrive early at the station.

IC Cards

Suica and Pasmo cards make it easier to get around, with seamless travel options.

  • Slurping noodles is a compliment to the chef.
  • Most places don't accept tips.
  • Try conveyor belt sushi and convenience store meals!
Conveyor Belt Sushi

Try fresh sushi served on conveyor belts — a fun dining experience!

Convenience Store Meals

Convenience stores in Japan offer surprisingly delicious meals.
