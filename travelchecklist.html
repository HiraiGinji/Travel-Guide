Hiking Gear

Pack sturdy shoes and weather-resistant clothes. Climbing up a mountain isnt easy! Better be prepared than be sorry later!

Layer up and bring a raincoat for unexpected weather. It gets very cold there too! So make sure you're all prepared for the big mean weather!

Essentials

Don’t forget your zoom lens, snacks, and water! You have no idea how long you'll be when you're up there! Pack at least 2 days worth of food
